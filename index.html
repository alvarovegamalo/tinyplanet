🪐 Tiny Planet Gardener

A cozy idle game for iPhone: grow a tiny planet into a living world, meet 14 adorable creatures, and watch your garden keep growing while you're away.

Un juego idle relajante para iPhone: convierte un planeta diminuto en un mundo lleno de vida.
